Activities and programme

ScotJam is about trying things you might not do on a normal meeting night, and doing them with new friends. The exact programme for 2027 will be confirmed closer to the events, and it can change on the day if the weather or staffing means we need to switch activities. What follows is based on recent ScotJam programmes, so families and leaders know the sort of week or weekend to expect.

The overall experience

Days are built around instructor-led sessions on land and water, with meals on site and an evening programme. Young people are usually in activity groups with others from their Scout Group and/or their accommodation block. Scout Adventures Lochgoilhead provides the specialist equipment needed for activities, such as helmets, harnesses and buoyancy aids. Participants should not bring their own activity equipment.

ScotJam: Beavers

In 2026, ScotJam: Beavers was a weekend at Lochgoilhead, from Friday to Sunday, with the option to visit for the day on the Saturday and Sunday. The weekend was designed for Beaver Colonies from across Scotland. Beavers took part in outdoor activities, team challenges and evening entertainment, stayed in indoor accommodation with meals provided (if staying overnight), and had the chance to work towards elements of the My Adventure Challenge Award and the My Outdoors Challenge Award.

In 2027, ScotJam: Beavers is from 4 to 6 June. The 2027 programme, and whether day visits are offered, will be confirmed here. More details coming soon.

ScotJam: Cubs

In recent years, ScotJam: Cubs has been a multi-day residential. Cubs have had a mix of water and land activities. In 2026 these were likely to include powerboat, hill walking, high ropes, mega stand-up paddleboarding, bushcraft, canoe or kayak, and rock climbing or abseiling. Earlier programmes have also included sailing or raft building.

A typical day has been breakfast, flag break into a morning activity, lunch, an afternoon activity, dinner, then early and late evening activities, with bedtime and site quiet in the evening. Meals are prepared on site. In 2026 the catering team was ESS-Compass, and meals were served on a rotation because of dining hall capacity. In 2027, ScotJam: Cubs is from 19 to 23 July. The 2027 daily shape, activities and catering arrangements will be confirmed here. More details coming soon.

There has usually been a disco on the final night. Cubs have been encouraged to bring bright, beach or tropical style clothing for it.

ScotJam: Scouts

In recent years, ScotJam: Scouts has been a week-long residential, with the programme in two parts.

First part of the week

The first part of the week has been taster sessions across land and water. In 2026 there were three taster programmes, because of the number of Scouts attending. These included a mix of kayaking, canoeing, paddleboarding, sailing, raft building, rock climbing, hill walking, high ropes, mountain biking, bushcraft and a burn run, plus an "Explorer Experience" option for one activity group. Scouts were assigned to an activity group.

Second half of the week

In the second half of the week, Scouts have chosen a pathway towards a badge or award. In 2026 the options included Nautical Skills, Sailing, Paddler, Survivor, Climber, or the Expedition Challenge (for those aged 12 and over). Some pathways have included a night away from the centre.

Evenings have included a choice of activities (in 2026 this included things such as Jacob's ladder, the climbing tower, crate stack, archery, games, and a campfire, a quieter night, and a final disco). In 2027, ScotJam: Scouts is from 10 to 17 July. The 2027 taster programmes, badge pathways and evening programme will be confirmed here. More details coming soon.

ScotJam: Explorers

ScotJam: Explorers returned as a summer residential in 2026, running for a week at Lochgoilhead. Public pages described a week of outdoor adventure, evening entertainment, and chances to work towards badges and awards, in indoor, fully catered accommodation. A detailed public Explorer programme was not available on the previous website when these pages were written.

In 2027, ScotJam: Explorers is from 25 to 31 July. A short description of the 2027 Explorer programme will be added here. More details coming soon.

Badges and awards

ScotJam is a chance to make progress on badges and awards, but the exact awards depend on the programme that year and on what the young person already has. We encourage young people to talk their experience through with their section leadership team after the event, so any badges can be awarded at the Group.

As a guide from recent events:

  • Beavers have had the chance to work towards elements of the My Adventure and My Outdoors Challenge Awards.
  • Cubs on recent programmes completed the requirements for the Our Adventure Challenge, and were likely to be eligible for activity badges such as Nautical Skills, Paddle Sports or Sailing, depending on their activity group, plus nights away and time on the water. Other challenge award requirements may also have been met.
  • Scouts have worked towards a chosen badge or challenge in the second half of the week, as set out above.

Which badges and awards will be offered in 2027, for each section, will be confirmed here. More details coming soon.

Evenings and time off

Evenings are part of the event, not an add-on. Recent programmes have included campfires, games, a disco and quieter options. There is also some down time. The kit list suggests a book, cards or a small travel game for those moments.
