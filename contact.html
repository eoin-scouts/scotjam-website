When is ScotJam 2027?

Beavers: 4 to 6 June 2027. Scouts: 10 to 17 July 2027. Cubs: 19 to 23 July 2027. Explorers: 25 to 31 July 2027.

Where is it?

Recent events have been at Scout Adventures Lochgoilhead, Lochgoilhead, Cairndow, PA24 8AQ. The 2027 venue will be confirmed here. More details coming soon.

Who can come?

Beaver Scouts, Cub Scouts, Scouts and Explorer Scouts, in their own section events. Whether booking is by group, by family, or both, will be confirmed for each section. More details coming soon.

How do I book, and what does it cost?

Booking opens: to be confirmed. Booking and fees are handled by Scouts Scotland, not on this website. When booking opens, we'll link to the official page from Home and from Info for leaders and parents.

Can my child come if they have additional needs, a medical condition or a special diet?

Yes, we want every young person to have a good event. Tell us what they need on the participant information form, and contact us if you'd like to talk it through beforehand. Give us dietary information in advance so catering can plan.

Will they be sleeping in tents?

No. On recent events, participants have stayed in indoor dorms or lodges, with meals provided.

Can they bring a phone?

Please leave phones and other devices at home, unless we have agreed a medical reason in advance. Any exception for a particular section in 2027 will be confirmed here. More details coming soon. We'll share some updates on official social channels, and we'll contact you if we need to.

What if they feel homesick?

That's common, and the team in their accommodation will support them. We'll contact you if we need to. It helps if you prepare them for a break from routine contact, and if you tell us what usually helps them.

How do I volunteer?

See the Volunteer with us page, and email scotjam@scouts.scot to register your interest until 2027 applications are open.

Who runs ScotJam?

ScotJam is a Scouts Scotland event, delivered with Scout Adventures Lochgoilhead and a volunteer team. Each section event has an Event Lead. The Lead for Events oversees the Event Leads.

How do I get in touch?

Email scotjam@scouts.scot. If you already have a booking, please include your ScotJam reference.

For official Scouts Scotland enquiries that are not about ScotJam, use the contacts on scouts.scot.

During an event, booked families will be given any extra contact instructions they need. We will not publish personal phone numbers on this page.

Privacy and charity information

ScotJam is a Scouts Scotland event. Scouts Scotland is a registered Scottish Charity No. SC017511.

We only ask for personal information when we need it to run the event, for example through official booking and participant information forms. You can read the Scouts Scotland privacy policy.
