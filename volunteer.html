Volunteer with us

ScotJam only happens because volunteers give their time. You'd be part of a team from across Scotland, supporting young people to have a safe, friendly and adventurous event.

There are roles before the event and during it, for adult volunteers and for Young Leaders. You don't need to have been to Lochgoilhead before.

Adult volunteer roles

Role titles and vacancy numbers will be confirmed for 2027. The roles below are the ones described on the previous ScotJam website, so you can see the sort of help we usually need.

  • Deputy Event Lead – works with the Event Lead from planning through to review, and helps oversee the team.
  • Welfare Team Lead – supports the Event Lead on participant welfare, reviews participant information, helps with accommodation and activity-group allocations, and supports the welfare team during the event. Some work is needed before the event starts.
  • Welfare Team Member – works with other volunteers to look after a group of young people in a dorm or lodge, including mealtimes, overnight, and when they are not on a supervised activity. This includes welcome, everyday support, help with medication following the event guidelines, and being a calm, visible adult.
  • Programme and Young Leader Coordinator – plans and delivers the evening programme (and, on recent Scout events, a quieter choice-based afternoon), and supports the Young Leader team. Some work is needed before the event.
  • Digital and Social Media Team Member – helps plan and post content, and gathers photos and film during the event, following media consent. Some work is needed before the event.
  • Transport Coordinator – helps plan coaches or minibuses. You may not need to be on site during the event, or you can combine this with another role.
  • Coach chaperone – an optional extra: travel with participants on a coach, including rest stops. Adult ratios on transport must follow The Scouts' Policy, Organisation and Rules (POR).
  • Beaver Team Member – helps welcome Beaver groups, support activities and run the evening programme, working with Group Leaders and the centre team.

The 2027 role list, descriptions and vacancy numbers for each event will be confirmed here. More details coming soon.

Adult volunteer requirements

To join the adult volunteer team you must:

  • Be 18 or over by the start of the event
  • Be an active member of the Scouts with an adult role
  • Have a valid disclosure (PVG) secured through Scouting
  • Have completed the required welcome, safety and safeguarding learning, recorded on the membership system
  • If you have held an adult appointment for more than six months, have completed the remaining Growing Roots learning for your role, and keep safety, safeguarding and first aid (if required for your role) in date through to the last day of the event

Applications are reviewed to check these requirements, and to find the best role for you. If you are new to the ScotJam team, we may offer a short welcome conversation before confirming a role.

All adult volunteers must agree to the ScotJam code of conduct. In summary: follow the Promise, Law and the values of Scouts; follow POR, the Yellow Card, and the Scouts guidance on alcohol; wear your ScotJam necker unless it has to come off for safety; be on time for duties; help other teams when asked; and follow direction from the Event Leadership Team and Scout Adventures staff. ScotJam is an alcohol-free event while young people are on site, in line with the centre's policy. There is no place for alcohol consumption at ScotJam.

Safeguarding concerns must be reported to the UK Headquarters Safeguarding Team, in line with the Yellow Card. Please also tell the Event Lead, unless the concern is about the Event Lead, in which case tell the Scout Adventures Lochgoilhead Centre Manager. Young Leaders are still young people, and they are included in these safeguarding responsibilities.

Young Leaders

Young Leaders help with the evening programme and with participant welfare, and may join in some activities. Spaces are limited, and the age rules can differ by event. On recent Scout events, Young Leaders have needed to be 16 or 17 years old. A PVG is required for Young Leaders who are 16 or 17 at the time of the event.

Successful Young Leaders have been invited to a Get Together before the event, to meet the team and prepare. If you apply, please include a parent or carer and/or your Explorer Scout Leader when you email us with questions.
