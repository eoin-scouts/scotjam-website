ScotJam

ScotJam is Scouts Scotland's national residential adventure for Beavers, Cubs, Scouts and Explorers, at Scout Adventures Lochgoilhead. The 2027 venue will be confirmed on this page. More details coming soon.

What is ScotJam?

ScotJam is a series of section events, not one week for every age group at once. Beavers, Cubs, Scouts and Explorers each have their own event, with a programme suited to their age and ability.

Young people come from across Scotland. They join instructor-led activities on land and water, take part in evening programmes, work towards badges and awards, and meet people they would not usually meet on a normal meeting night. Activities are run by the Scout Adventures team and by trained volunteers, with the right qualifications and permits in place.

In 2027, ScotJam: Beavers is a weekend, from 4 to 6 June. ScotJam: Scouts is from 10 to 17 July, ScotJam: Cubs from 19 to 23 July, and ScotJam: Explorers from 25 to 31 July, with indoor, fully catered accommodation. Whether Beavers will also have a day-visit option in 2027 will be confirmed here. More details coming soon.

ScotJam is a Scouts Scotland event, delivered with Scout Adventures Lochgoilhead and a volunteer team. Event Leads look after each section event, and the Lead for Events oversees that work.

Who is it for?

  • Beaver Scouts, Cub Scouts, Scouts and Explorer Scouts in Scotland.
  • Parents and carers who want to know what to pack, how travel works, and how we will keep in touch.
  • Leaders and groups supporting young people to attend.
  • Adult volunteers and Young Leaders who want to help run the events.

How places are booked in 2027, including whether a parent or carer can book an individual place and whether some sections stay as group bookings, will be confirmed here. More details coming soon.
